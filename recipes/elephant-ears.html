Elephant Ears

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup Warm Water
  • 1 package Yeast
  • 2 tbs Milk
  • 2 tbs Sugar
  • 1/4 cup Vegetable Oil
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 6 1/2 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Mix water, yeast, sugar, and milk until yeast dissolves
  2. Add oil, baking powder, salt, and flour. Knead until soft.
  3. Roll into flat round pieces
  4. Cook in oil at 350F
  5. Butter with Cinnamon sugar

EAT AND ENJOY =)

Quick Info

  • Oil temp 350F
