Honey Siracha Chicken

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 4 Chicken Breast
  • 1/2 c Catsup
  • 1/4 c Honey
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1/4 c Sriracha Sauce
  • 1/2 c Water
  • 3 tsp Cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Place chicken in pot
  2. in bowl whisk the catsup, sriracha, honey, soy sauce, and water until smooth.
  3. Close the lid and set the valve to sealing cook on high pressure for 10 minutes.
  4. Once the countdown has ended, CAREFULLY do a quick pressure release. This ensures the chicken wont over cook.
  5. Take the chicken out and cut into bite size pieces.
  6. Press the saute button on the Instant Pot. In small bowl mix the cornstarch with 1/4 cup of water and add to the Instant Pot, simmer until thickened.
  7. Add chicken back when the sauce has thickened.
  8. Serve over cooked rice.
