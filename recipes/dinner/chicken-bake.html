Chicken Bake

Prep Time: Total Time: 55 minutes Servings:

Ingredients

  • 1 c Shredded Mozzarella
  • 1 c Shredded Provolone
  • 1/4 c Grated Parmesan
  • 2 c Skinless Chopped Rotisserie Chicken Meat
  • 4 slices Cooked and Crumbled Bacon
  • 2 tbsp Chopped Fresh Chives
  • 1/3 c Good Quality Prepared Creamy Caesar Dressing
  • Freshly Ground Black Pepper
  • 1 pound Pizza Dough at Room Temperature
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil for the Dough
  • 1 tbsp Unsalted Butter, melted and cooled

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375f and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Combine the Mozzarella, Provolone, Parmesan in a large bowl and toss to combine. Transfer 1/2 c of the cheese mixture to a small bowl and reserve.
  3. Add chicken, bacon and chives to the large bowl with the cheese. Drizzle with the Caesar dressing and season with several grinds of black pepper. Toss until everything is combined and well coated.
  4. Divide the pizza dough into 4 equal pieces and rub lightly with olive oil. Roll one piece into a 7 by 9 inch rectangle.
  5. With one of the long sides closest to you, arrange a quarter of the filling in a log across the center of the rectangle, leaving an inch at each side.
  6. Fold in the sides, then fold the bottom piece of the dough over the filling and roll up to to enclose it, trimming away any excess dough.
  7. Pinch the seams together to seal well.
  8. Place the roll, seam side down, on the prepared baking sheet.
  9. Repeat with the remaining dough and filling, leaving about 2 inches between each on the baking sheet.
  10. Brush the rolls all over with the melted butter and sprinkle with the reserved cheese mixture.
  11. Bake until rolls are golden brown and the cheese topping is crusty and browned, 25-30 minutes.
  12. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.
