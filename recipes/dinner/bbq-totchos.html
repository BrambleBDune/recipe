BBQ Totchoes

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 8 strips bacon
  • 30 oz Crispy Crowns or Tater Tots
  • Chicken breast
  • 1/2 tsp each salt and pepper tsp each garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika
  • Colby Jack Cheese
  • Buffalo Wild Wing's Honey BBQ sauce Ranch dressing
  • 2 chopped green onions

Instructions

  1. Heat a griddle over medium high heat. Cook the bacon until crispy, remove it from the griddle, and spread the bacon grease over the griddle.
  2. Add the bag of crispy crowns to the griddle and let them cook until they are golden brown and crispy. Keep flipping them as they cook.
  3. Cut the chicken into cubes and add it to the griddle. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika. Once the chicken is fully cooked, turn off the heat and add some Buffalo Wild VWings Honey BBQ sauce to the chicken and stir well.
  4. Top the crispy crowns with Colby Jack cheese and cover with a griddle dome until the cheese is melted.
  5. Top the cheesy crispy crowns with the bbq chicken, ranch dressing, crispy bacon, and chopped green onions. Enjoy!
