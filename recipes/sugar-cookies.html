Sugar Cookies

Prep Time: Cook Time: 6-8 minutes Servings:

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup Shortening
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 Egg
  • 4 tsp milk
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Thoroughly cream, shortening, sugar, vanilla, add egg, beat until light and fluffy, stir in milk
  2. Stir together dry ingredients
  3. Blend into creamed mix
  5. Divide in 1/2 and chill for 1 hour
  6. On lightly floured surface roll into 1/8 inch thickness
  7. Bake in greased sheet at 375 F for 6-8 minutes
  8. Let cool slightly then remove and decorate

Quick Info

  • Oven Temp 375 F
  • Bake Time 6-8 minutes
