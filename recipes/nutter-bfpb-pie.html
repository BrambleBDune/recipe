Nutter Butter Frozen PB Pie

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • Crust
  • 24 Nutter Butters
  • 5 Tbs Butter Melted
  • Filling
  • 1 Package (8 oz) Cream Cheese softened
  • 1 cup Creamy Peanut Butter
  • 3/4 cup Sugar
  • 1 tbs Vanilla
  • 1 tub (8 oz) Cool Whip thawed divided

Instructions

  1. Crush cookies in ziplock bag or in food processor mix crumbs and butter. Press onto the bottom and sides of a 9 inch pie plate.
  2. Mix cream cheese, peanut butter, sugar, and vanilla until well blended, gently stir in 1 1/2 whipped topping spoon onto crust
  3. Freeze for 4 hours or overnight until firm. Let stand for 30 minutes or until the pie can be cut easily
  4. Top with the remaining whipped topping and additional cookies.
  5. Special Extra: Just before serving drizzle chocolate syrup on plates.

Quick Info

  • Freeze for 4 hours
  • Let stand 30 minutes or until easily cut
