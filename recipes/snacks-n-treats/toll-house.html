Toll House Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 c Flour
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 3/4 c sugar
  • 1 tsp Vanilla
  • 1 2/3 (11 oz) Chocolate Chips (Or even better measure that shit with your heart)
  • 1 tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 c Margarine
  • 3/4 Brown Sugar
  • 2 Large Eggs
  • 1 c Chopped Nuts (Very Optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375f
  2. Combine dry ingredients in a bowl
  3. Beat sugars, butter, and vanilla till creamy. Add eggs and beat till smooth
  4. Gradually add dry ingredients and then fold in nuts and morsels
  5. Bake for 9-11 minutes

Quick Info

  • Oven temp 375f
  • Bake for 9-11 minutes
