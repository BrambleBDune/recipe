Weekend Chili

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 1 tbs Olive Oil
  • 2 lbs Chuck Steak Cubed
  • 1 lb Crumbled Hot Italian Sausage
  • 2 large Onions
  • 12 Cloves Garlic Diced
  • 1 tbs Cumin
  • 1 tbs Paprika
  • 1 tbs Chili Pepper
  • 1 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup Water
  • 1 15 oz can Pinto Beans
  • 1 15 oz can Northern Beans
  • 1 15 oz can Dark Kidney Beans

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in large Dutch oven over medium heat.
  2. Brown steak and sausage for 8 minutes.
  3. Transfer to a bowl reserving 2 tbs of fat from the meat and discard the rest.
  4. Saute garlic and onions in the reserved fat until translucent about 8 minutes.
  5. Add cumin, paprika, and chili powder. Cook for 1 minute.
  6. Add tomatoes, water, sausage and beef. Stir and simmer for 90 minutes.
  7. Rinse and drain the beans the add them.
  8. Simmer for 30 minutes
  9. Pour into bowls and garnish
