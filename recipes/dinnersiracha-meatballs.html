Sriracha Meatballs

Prep Time: Cook Time:2-3 hours Servings:

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Meatballs
  • 1/3 - 1/2 cup Sriracha
  • 1 can Jellied Cranberry Sauce
  • 1/2 Brown Sugar
  • 1/3 cup Honey

Instructions

  1. Put meatballs in slow cooker
  2. Heat the reat of the ingredients in a saucepan over medium heat until everthing is melted and disolved.
  3. Dump Sauce over the meatballs and stir to coat meatballs
  4. Cook on low for 2-3 hours stirring occasionally
