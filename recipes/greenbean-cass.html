Green Bean Casserole

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 1 can (10 3/4oz) Cream Of Mushroom
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • 1 tsp Soy Sauce
  • Dash of Pepper
  • 4 cup Cooked Cut Green Beans
  • 1 1/3 cup Frenches French Fried Onions

Instructions

  1. Mix soup, milk. soy sauce, pepper, beans and 2/3 cups onions in 1 1/2 quart casserole pan
  2. Bake at 350f for 25 minutes
  3. Stir and sprinkle with remaining onions
