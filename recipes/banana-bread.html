Banana Bread

Prep Time: Cook Time: Servings:

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cup Sugar
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1/2 cup Butter or Margarine
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 1/2 Mashed Banana (Very Ripe)
  • 1/2 cups Buttermilk
  • 1 tsp Vanilla
  • 2 1/2 cups Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 cup Chopped Nuts (Optional)

Instructions

  1. Move oven rack to the low position so that the top of the pan is in the center of the oven. Heat to 350F Grease bottoms only of 2 loaf pans
  2. Mix sugar and butter in large bowl. Stir in eggs until blended. Add bananas, buttermilk and vanilla and beat until smooth. Stir in flour baking soda and salt just until moist. Stir in nuts, then pour in the pan.
  3. Bake 8 inch loaves for about 1 hour, 9 inch loaves 1 1/4 hour or until a toothpick come out clean.
  4. Place upside down on rack
  5. Cool for 2 hours

Quick Info

  • Oven temp 350F
  • 8 inch loaf 1 hour
  • 9 inch loaf 1 1/4 hours
