Sweet and Sour

Prep Time: Cook Time: 1 hour Servings:

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Beaten Egg
  • Flour
  • 1 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1 cup Crushed Pineapple
  • 1 cup Vinegar
  • 1 cup Chicken Broth
  • 2 tbs Soy Sauce
  • 8 tbs Catsup
  • 1/2 - 1 tbs Paprika
  • 3 tbs Cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Dip chicken in beaten egg and flour then brown lightly.
  2. Bring remaining ingredients to a boil and cook until clear then pour over chicken.
  3. Bake at 350F for 1 hour
  4. Serve over rice

Quick Info

  • Bake at 350F for 1 hour
