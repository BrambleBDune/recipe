Lemon Bars

Prep Time: Cook Time:45-50 mins Servings:30 Bars

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Sifted Flour
  • 2 cups Sugar
  • 1/2 cups Sifted Powder Sugar
  • 1/3 cups Lemon Juice
  • 1 cup Butter
  • 1/4 cups Flour
  • 4 Eggs
  • 2 1/2 tbsp Baking Powder

Instructions

  1. Sift together Sifted flour and sifted sugar
  2. Cut in butter till the mixture clings together
  3. Press into a 13x9 2 inch deep baking pan
  4. Bake at 350 F till lightly browned, about 20-25 min
  5. Put eggs, 2 cups sugar, and lemon juice in a blender and blend until thick and smooth
  6. Sift 1/4 cup flour and baking powder. Add to blender, blend to combine. Pour over crust.
  7. Bake at 350 F for 25 mins
  8. Sprinkle with sugar
  9. Let cool and cut into bars

Quick Info

  • Oven Temp 350 F
