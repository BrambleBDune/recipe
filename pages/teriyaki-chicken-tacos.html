Teriyaki Chicken Tacos

Prep Time: Cook Time: 11 Mins Servings:

Ingredients

    Creamy Cucumber

  • 1/4 cup Plain Greek Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Sweet Chili Sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted Sesame Oil
  • 1 tsp Chili Crunch
  • 1/4 tsp Salt
  • 1 cup Fresh Herbs, Finely Minced (Cilantro, Green Onions and/or Mint
  • 1/2 tsp Sesame Seeds
  • 1 Large English Cucumber or 3*4 Persian Cucumbers, Thinly Sliced

    • Teriyaki Chicken

  • 1lbs Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs
  • Pinches of Salt and Pepper
  • 1/3 cup + 1/4 cup Teriyaki Sauce
  • 2 tbsp Sweet Chili Sauce

    • For Serving

  • 8 6in Warmed Flour Tortillas
  • 1 Sliced Avacado
  • 1/4 cup Crushed Peanuts
  • Sesame Seeds

Instructions

    Chicken Part 1

  1. Season the chicken thighs on both sides with big pinches of salt anf pepper.
  2. Place them in an Instant Pot and add 1/3 cup teriyaki sauce
  3. Toss the chicken to fully coat each thigh with sauce.
  4. Place the lid on the Instant Pot and set it to sealing.
  5. Set the pot to pressure cook for ten minutes.

    6. Creamy Cucumbers

  6. Thinly slice cucumbers (***BE VERY CAREFUL*** I like to use a Mandolin for this ***BE VERY CAREFUL***)
  7. Whisk together the dressing
  8. Combine greek yogurt, mayo, rice vinegar, sweet chili sauce, sesame oil, chili crunch, and salt until well combined.
  9. Toss the dressing with the cucumbers and the fresh herbs.
  10. Reserve a couple tablespoons of dressing to drizzle on the finished tacos.

    11. Chicken Part 2

  11. Natrual or Quick release the pressure from the Instant Pot being VERY careful of the steam
  12. Remove and shred the chicken
  13. Place the chicken on a lined baking sheet and preheat the oven to broil
  14. Toss the chicken in the remaining 1/4 cup of teriyaki sauce and the 2 tablespoons sweet chili sauce
  15. Broil the chicken for 3-6 minutes until the chicken is crispy and carmelized around the edges ***WATCH CLOSELY BECAUSE ALL OVENS ARE DIFFERNT***
  16. Remove the chicken
