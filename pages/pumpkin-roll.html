Sourdough Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep Time: Cook Time: 15 minutes Servings:

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cups Sifted Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder
  • 2 tps Ground Cinnamon
  • 1 tsp Pumkin Pie Spice
  • 1/2 tsp Ground Nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 3 Eggs
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 2/3 cup Canned Pumkin
  • 1 cup Chopped Walnuts

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to to 375 F. Grease 15x10x1 jelly roll pan lined with wax paper
  2. Sift flour, baking powder, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice, nutmeg, and salt together
  3. Beat eggs and sugar together until light and fluffy, beat in pumpkin, stir in sifted dry ingredients all at once, pour into pan and spread evenly
  4. Bake 15 minutes or until the center springs back when lightly touched
  5. Loosen edges with a knife and invert onto towel dusted with powdered sugar and cool completely
  6. Unroll and spread filling, reroll and put in fridge untill ready to serve

    7. Filling

  7. Beat together: 1 cup sifted powdered sugar, 8 oz cream cheese, 6 tbs butter, 1 tps vanilla

Quick Info

  • Oven Temp 375 F
