Classic Barbecue Pork Ribs

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 2 hours Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 racks of pork ribs
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
  2. Mix brown sugar, paprika, and garlic powder; rub over the ribs.
  3. Wrap ribs in foil and bake for 1.5 hours.
  4. Unwrap, brush with barbecue sauce, and bake uncovered for 30 minutes.
